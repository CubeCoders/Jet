# Contributing to Jet

Contributions are welcome — bug reports, fixes, new platforms, new examples,
documentation improvements, all of it. Please open an issue or pull request
on the official repository.

## Contribution licensing

contribution, you agree to make it available under the same licence.
No separate Contributor Licence Agreement is required.

You retain copyright in your contributions. Please only submit work that
you have the right to contribute under MIT, including any necessary
permission from your employer or other copyright holders. Preserve any
applicable third-party copyright and licence notices.
